How to read this page

What do "likely to rise" and "likely to fall" mean?

The page looks at the price trend, recent news, and how this asset has behaved in the past, and makes a best guess about the direction. "Likely to rise" means a rise is the more probable outcome. It is a lean, not a certainty.

What do "slight", "fair" and "strong" mean?

How confident the guess is. Slight means barely better than a coin toss. Fair means a clear lean. Strong means a firm lean. Even a strong call can be wrong; it just happens less often.

Why does it so often say "could go either way"?

Because short-term prices are hard to predict, and the page says so instead of pretending. Any website that claims it knows where prices go next is guessing or selling something.

Where does the gold price come from?

From the world gold price, converted to rupees at today’s exchange rate and into Indian weights. Import duty and GST are added to get the India price. Your jeweller adds making charges, so the shop price will be a bit higher.

Is the "price abroad" useful to me?

It shows how much of the India price is tax. The gap between the two numbers is duty plus GST.

Should I buy or sell based on this?

No. Use it to understand what the data is pointing to and check the track record to see how often it is right. Decide with your own judgement, and never put in money you cannot afford to lose.
