How to read this page

What does "62% likely up" mean?

The model thinks there is a 62 in 100 chance the price is higher at the end of that period than it is now. It is not a promise. Even a good call at 62% is wrong about 4 times in 10.

Why is almost everything close to 50%?

Because short-term prices are mostly unpredictable. A system that was right 70% of the time on daily moves would be the best in the world. Numbers near 50% mean "no clear signal", and the page says so instead of guessing.

What is "momentum"?

A measure called RSI, from 0 to 100, of how hard the price has been pushed recently. Above 70 means it has risen fast and may be stretched; below 30 means it has fallen fast and may be oversold. Between 30 and 70 is normal.

Where do the gold and silver prices come from?

From the world price in US dollars per ounce, converted to rupees at today's exchange rate and into Indian units. The "India" figure adds import duty and GST. Jewellers add making charges, so shop prices are higher.

How is the outlook calculated?

Four things: the price trend (technical), a statistical model trained on that asset's own history (quantitative), today's headlines scored as positive or negative (sentiment), and any earnings or valuation news (fundamental). They are combined into one probability per period.

Should I buy or sell based on this?

No. Use it to understand what the data is pointing to and to check how often it is right over time. Decide with your own judgement, and never put in money you cannot afford to lose.
